👤 About Digitera Levi

Hi! I'm Levi, the creator behind Digitera Levi Shop — your go-to source for premium no-code digital business tools and assets.

I started this shop with one goal: to help everyday entrepreneurs — even those without tech backgrounds — build and scale their online businesses using done-for-you digital tools.

From funnel builders and shop systems to Canva templates, PLR products, game apps, and inventory managers — everything here is designed to save you time, eliminate guesswork, and help you earn faster.

My promise to you: Every product I sell is something I've built, used, or tested myself. You're getting real tools, not just pretty templates.

Thank you for trusting Digitera Levi. Let's build your digital empire together! 🚀

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How will I receive my purchase?

After successful payment, an email with your download link will be sent automatically. Please check your spam/promotions folder.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept GCash, Maya, Credit/Debit Card, and QR Ph — all powered by PayMongo.

Can I resell these products?

Products with Master Resell Rights (MRR) or PLR can be resold. Please check the product description for specific licensing terms.

Do you offer refunds?

Due to the digital nature of our products, all sales are final. However, if you experience technical issues, please contact us.

Do I need technical skills?

No! All our products are designed for non-techies. Ready to use out of the box.

How do I use a coupon code?

Add items to cart, then enter your coupon code in the "Have a coupon?" field before checkout.

How can I reach you?

Click the Contact tab or message us on Facebook Messenger. We respond within 24 hours!
